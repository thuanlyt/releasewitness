Dogfood closeout · OSBlog

From a real workload
to better control.

ReleaseWitness supervised a bilingual open-source blog through planning, worker fallbacks, audits, quota failure, takeover, QA and Vercel release evidence. This page keeps the successes and the friction visible.

Production boundary

OSBlog's live target for this case study is Vercel: osblog.thuanlyt.id.vn and osblog.vercel.app. VPS, Netlify and local Node are documented targets, not live OSBlog environments.

What was actually evidenced

01Planning and dispatch

A light goal and roster became scoped work items, mailboxes and copyable worker prompts.

02Failure and recovery

Unavailable runtimes, quota exhaustion, no-report attempts and takeover history remained visible instead of being rewritten as success.

03Audit to fix

Independent review reproduced defects; workers added fixes and regressions; a second review checked the result.

04Live release boundary

Vercel aliases, SSR/SEO routes, feeds, sitemap, robots and existing Cap media have recorded smoke evidence.

Timeline

  1. Bootstrap.

  2. Fallback.

  3. Review.

  4. Release.

    Vercel and recovery evidence accumulated, while the positive historical-308 fixture stayed intentionally uncreated.

  5. Takeover.

    UA-0091 exhausted quota; UA-0093 resumed from a partial checkpoint and passed its focused security gate.

Findings that changed the product conversation

The strongest result was not task count. It was the ability to distinguish live, local, simulation and blocked evidence.

Shipped: evidence and recovery

UA-0048 through UA-0051 now provide report freshness, typed provenance, takeover lineage and bounded sanitized evidence with raw diagnostics kept in a local spool.

Shipped: source and runtime gates

UA-0052 through UA-0055 bind QA and release durability to source state, default QA to safe argv execution, and worker intake to bounded readiness and failure classification.

Residual: higher assurance

Capture verification, stale-lock recovery, filesystem diff enforcement and authenticated remote identity remain backlog. They are not blockers for the documented trusted-local ReleaseWitness model.

2026-09 redundancy validation

The OSBlog dogfood run showed why durable evidence could matter, but it also exposed orchestration overhead. After Claude Code gained stronger native subagents, model routing, resume behavior and worktree isolation, RelWit retested its own reason to exist instead of assuming the old UseAgent design was still necessary.

56Real Claude Code sessions

Opus 5.5 leads/reviewers and Sonnet 5.5 workers were verified from runtime model-usage records; runs included held-out tests, hard kills, max-turn failures, stale-QA drift and worktree concurrency.

N0Native Claude matched the final outcomes

Across the tested fixture, native execution, native + RelWit assurance and full RelWit supervision all ended with passing held-out acceptance and zero false READY decisions.

N2Full supervision added overhead

It cost 1.9–4.8× more per task and took 1.8–5.6× longer in the measured runs, with extra cancelled/blocked work items and one misleading failed-attempt record.

COREWhat survived

The remaining product case is the smaller provider-neutral source gate: relwit qa plus relwit gate. The supervision machinery is deprecated pending owner decision.

Tóm tắt bằng tiếng Việt

OSBlog là workload thật để kiểm chứng ReleaseWitness. Supervisor đã tạo task/DAG, dispatch cho Codex, Claude và Antigravity-style identities, giữ failure/quota history, đọc report, audit, QA, takeover và evidence Vercel. Media Cap cũ được giữ vì đã có provenance; capture mới bị block vì không xác minh được browser target.

Finding quan trọng nhất là repository evidence phải có provenance và source identity rõ. ReleaseWitness hiện đã ship report freshness, typed evidence, takeover lineage, evidence hygiene, source-bound QA, Git durability, safe QA và runtime readiness; sandbox, identity xác thực và cycle metrics vẫn là backlog.
